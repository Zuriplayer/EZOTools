# EZOTools

Quick command panel for keyboard, mouse and gamepad in *The Elder Scrolls Online*.

¿Prefieres español? Lee el [README en español](README.es.md).
📢 For support, feedback, bug reports or suggestions, join our Discord: https://discord.gg/ekw8zUAcRm

## ✨ What it does

A quick command panel and small on-screen overlay, for keyboard, mouse and gamepad:

- Travel to your house, crafting hall or secondary hall
- Jump to the group leader, leave group/instance
- Repair your gear or recharge weapons when something is actually low
- Recall your last mount, pet, companion or assistant, with recent history
- Track your food/drink buff and reuse it with one click
- Return to your own or visited houses

## 🎮 How to open it

- **Mouse**: click on the overlay, or right-click for the recents menu
- **Gamepad/Keyboard**: hold X for commands, hold Y for quick utilities (check Controls for the exact keybind)
- **Chat**: `/ezo help` for commands, `/ezo about` for contact info

## 🌍 Language & settings

Spanish and English, auto-detected from your client (can be forced in settings). Quick settings from the panel itself, full settings via LibAddonMenu.

## Requirements

- The Elder Scrolls Online (PC)
- [LibAddonMenu-2.0](https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info7-LibAddonMenu.html) (required)
- Optional: LibChatMessage, LibDebugLogger, DebugLogViewer, LibSlashCommander

## Installation

1. Download the latest version from [Releases](../../releases) (or clone this repository).
2. Copy the `EZOTools` folder into your ESO AddOns folder:
   `Documents/Elder Scrolls Online/live/AddOns/`
3. Make sure LibAddonMenu-2.0 is also installed.
4. Enable the addon from the in-game Add-Ons screen.
